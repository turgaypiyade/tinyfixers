How it plays

Every level you beat fixes something real

A classic match-3 at heart, with a reason to keep going: each win brings a famous landmark one step closer to its old glory.

1

Match

Swap tiles to make lines of three or more. Bigger matches build rockets, PatchBots and Pulse Cores.

2

Earn stars

Clear the level's goals: crates, mud, gel, statues under tarps. Every hand-made level you win pays out stars.

3

Repair a wonder

Spend stars on repair tasks: weld the columns, polish the marble, raise the flag. Finish them all and the wonder shines again.

The toolbox

Tiles, specials and jokers

Four colors, one rule: line up three or more of the same tile. Make bigger shapes and you get a special.

Specials

Match 4 in a line

Rocket

Clears a whole row or column.

2×2 square

Patchbot

Flies off and hits a target on the board.

Match 5 in an L or T

PulseCore

Blasts the 5×5 area around it.

Match 5 in a line

Spark Orb

Clears every tile of one color.

Swap two specials into each other for an even bigger combo.

Jokers

Single target

Wonder Hammer

Tap any tile or obstacle to break it.

Row

Wonder Drill

Clears the row you tap.

Column

Wonder Elevator

Clears the column you tap.

Whole board

Wonder Shuffler

Shuffles every tile on the board.

One color, 3 moves

Wonder Paint Bucket

Tap a tile: that color leaves the board for 3 moves.
