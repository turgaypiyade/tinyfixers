Delete your Wonder Fixers account

Wondi Games · Julvera Teknoloji A.Ş.

You can delete your Wonder Fixers account and the data linked to it at any time.

In the game (fastest)

  1. Open Settings (gear icon on the home screen).
  2. Tap Terms & Privacy.
  3. Tap Delete My Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and the game restarts with a fresh account.

Without the game

If you no longer have the game installed, email hello@wondigames.com with the subject "Account deletion" and include your friend code (shown on your profile in the game). We will delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account and player profile (name, avatar, friend code)
  • Your saved progress, coins, boosters and in-game items
  • Your team membership and team chat messages
  • Your leaderboard entries and gameplay statistics

What we may keep

  • Purchase records that we must keep by law (for example, for tax purposes), for the period required by law.
  • Reports you sent about other players, which do not include your profile.
  • Aggregated analytics that no longer identify you (deleted from Firebase Analytics within 14 months).

See our Privacy Policy for more details.
